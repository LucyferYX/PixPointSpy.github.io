Pixel Spotting Game

A "spot the difference" game about finding a single pixel that differs between two images.


How to play

  • Left-click the altered pixel on the right image to advance to the next level.
  • Right-click the image to zoom in the image.
  • Use hints to reveal section the pixel is located at.
  • Each level increases difficulty as the pixel grid becomes smaller.
  • Level, time, mistakes and hints used determine your final score.

Challenge Yourself


The game fetches from over 1000 random images and selects a different pixel every time you play.
